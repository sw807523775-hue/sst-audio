1The lecture is about several ways to use and interpret the word need.
讲座是关于使用和解释“need”一词的几种方式。
2Firstly, saying I need a holiday is an expression of a strong desire but not a basic need.
首先，“我需要一个假期”表达的是一种强烈的愿望，而非基本需求。
3Furthermore, he mentions that the plant needs water is an objective statement, because it is essential, like sunlight and water to plants.
此外，他提到“植物需要水”是一个客观陈述，因为水对植物来说是必不可少的，如同阳光和水。
4Finally, there is a psychological use when someone lacks something.
最后，还有一种当某人缺乏某物时的心理用法。
