1The lecture is about melatonin.
讲座是关于褪黑素的。
2Firstly, it is produced in the pineal gland, the size of a pea, in the brain.
首先，它在大脑中豌豆大小的松果体内产生。
3Furthermore, he mentions this hormone has a rhythm and is the opposite of cortisol.
此外，他提到这种激素有节律，与皮质醇相反。
4It peaks at night, called the darkness hormone.
它在夜间达到峰值，被称为“黑暗激素”。
5In conclusion, melatonin occurs at night and prepares you for night activities, including sleep in humans, showing its role in darkness behavior.
总之，褪黑素在夜间产生，为你夜间的活动做准备，包括人类的睡眠，显示出它在黑暗相关行为中的作用。
